Your two ideas are not necessarily bad, but both are capital-intensive, slow to monetise, and legally sensitive. The main issue is that you are trying to build products before proving that people will pay for a narrow outcome.

I would not launch an app that tells SMSF users which ASX shares to buy and sell. Recommending specific shares or influencing people to trade financial products can require an Australian financial services licence or operation as an authorised representative. ASIC is actively targeting unlicensed “finfluencer” activity, especially claims involving easy or guaranteed returns. (asic.gov.au)

Also, an 18% return every three months is not a sensible product promise. Even if your strategy works in a particular period, marketing it as a target or expected result creates major legal, trust, and financial-risk problems. ASIC specifically warns investors to question high returns and says there is no guaranteed investment return other than certain bank products. (asic.gov.au)

Privacy Strategy
You do not need to collect much personal data for an MVP.

Start with:

Email
Password or passwordless login
Watchlists
Saved searches
Payment information handled by Stripe or another provider
No TFNs
No bank credentials
No super fund account data
No identity documents
No brokerage credentials
No direct trade execution
Australian privacy obligations depend on your business structure and activities. Many small businesses under $3 million turnover are not automatically covered, but exceptions apply, including trading in personal information, residential tenancy databases, health data, and Consumer Data Right activities. If covered, the Australian Privacy Principles govern collection, use, disclosure, security, access, and correction of personal information. (oaic.gov.au)

For your MVP, implement:

Privacy policy
Terms of use
Data deletion function
Minimal data collection
Encryption in transit and at rest
No selling user data
Clear third-party data-source disclosures
Backups and access logging
Separate production and development databases
